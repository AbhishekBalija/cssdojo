# Fonts

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and were downloaded from Google Fonts (latin subset).

- Onest by Dmitri Voloshin: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Onest
- Martian Mono by Evil Martians: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Martian+Mono
- Instrument Serif by Instrument: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif

They are bundled so the scorer and the play screen render text identically, with no network.
